A cup of tea

One chart about making tea, drawn as tags on a kitchen shelf. Each state is a paper tag, each step is a line of ink, and the one way it goes wrong is traced in red.

How it was made

  1. Pick the world. Tea lives in a kitchen, so each state is a paper tag on a shelf.
  2. Draw the structure. Every state and step in the chart is on the plate, and nothing else is.
  3. Keep red for meaning. The broken path and the state it reaches are red; everything else is ink.
  • Every cup was steeped. Steeped
  • No cup steeps longer than five minutes. NotBitter
